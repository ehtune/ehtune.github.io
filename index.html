EH/tune

Tuscany University Network in Economic History

EH/tune brings together the economic historians working in Tuscany's universities and research institutes. We organise seminars, workshops and an annual conference, support doctoral students and early-career researchers, and build shared research projects on the long-run economic history of Italy, Europe and the wider world.

About the network Past workshops

Four partner institutions in three Tuscan cities. Meet the members

Our history

EH/tune grew out of years of informal collaboration among economic historians in Tuscany, many of whom already teach together in the joint PhD in Economics of the Tuscan Universities offered by Siena, Florence and Pisa.

The network gives that collaboration a permanent home: a common calendar of seminars, a meeting place for faculty and doctoral students across campuses, and a platform for research projects too large for any single department.

Tuscany holds some of the richest accounting archives in Europe. This track brings together members
who reconstruct prices, wages and consumption from them, and shares the resulting series in an open dataset.

## Questions

- How did living standards in Tuscan cities compare with the rest of Europe between 1300 and 1900?
- What explains the long divergence between northern and southern Europe?

## Get involved

Doctoral students and researchers working with similar sources are welcome to join the track's
working group, which meets each term.

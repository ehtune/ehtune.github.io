## Papers

- Gabriele Cappelli (UniSi), Alemu Lambamo (Oxford) and Michelangelo Vasta (UniSi), *Italy's military occupation and early mass schooling in Ethiopia: evidence from a new dataset*
- Valerio Cerretano (UniFi), *Placing Italian autarky in the international context. The drain in foreign exchange receipts, import substitution and corporate capitalism in the Italian experience of economic self-sufficiency, 1931–1947c.*
- Giacomo Caserta, Carlo Ciccarelli (Rome Tor Vergata) and Giuliana Freschi (SSSA), *Demography at work: Industrial productivity and age structure in Liberal Italy*
- Laura Hastings Mela (UniSi), *Geography of Power: Electrification, Industrial Location and Labor Conflict in Italy (1878–1911)*
- Marco Martinez and Valeria Pinchera (UniPi), *Trademarking Innovation: Exploring the Role of Intellectual Property in Italy's Traditional Sectors, 1861–1939*
- Alessandro Brioschi (SSSA), *Saint Roch Playing Favourites? Plague, Skill, and Wage Inequality in Early Modern Venice*

Introduction, chair and wrap-up: Alessandro Nuvolari (SSSA). Chair of the afternoon session: Valerio Cerretano (UniFi).
The programme also included a short introduction to the Domus Mazziniana by Pietro Finelli.

The workshop assessed the place of Italian economic history within the international debates of the
discipline, and the relevance of the Italian case to them. Each speaker presented a short review,
discussed informally.

## Programme

**Session I** (chair: Giandomenico Piluso, University of Siena)

- Growth and development: Giovanni Vecchi (University of Rome Tor Vergata)
- Technology: Alessandro Nuvolari (Sant'Anna School of Advanced Studies, Pisa)
- Institutions and rules: Paolo Di Martino (University of Birmingham)

**Session II** (chair: Leandro Conte, University of Siena)

- Business history: Andrea Colli (Bocconi University, Milan)
- Sources for economic history: Michelangelo Vasta (University of Siena)

**Final discussion** (chair: Emanuele Felice, University of Siena)

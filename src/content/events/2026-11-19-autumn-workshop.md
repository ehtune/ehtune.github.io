The Autumn Workshop is the network's annual meeting for doctoral students in economic history.
Each paper is assigned a faculty discussant from a different institution.

## Programme

**Thursday 19 November**

- 14:00 Welcome
- 14:30 Session 1: Prices, wages and living standards
- 16:30 Session 2: Banking and public finance

**Friday 20 November**

- 09:30 Session 3: Trade and market integration
- 11:30 Keynote lecture
- 13:00 Close
